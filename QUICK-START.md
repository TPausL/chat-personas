## 💬 What to Ask

Your persona handles everything - just ask naturally:

### Exercises
```
"I'm stuck on problem 3. Here's what I tried..."
"Explain this concept from lecture 5"
"Is my proof approach correct?"
```

### Planning
```
"Plan my study week for this course"
"I have 3 deadlines this week - help me prioritize"
"Create an 8-week exam prep plan"
```

### Exam Prep
```
"Give me practice questions on [topic]"
"Conduct a mock oral exam"
"What are the key concepts I should focus on?"
```

## 🎯 Pro Tips

1. **Be specific** - "I'm stuck on problem 3, part b" works better than "I don't get it"
2. **Show your work** - "Here's what I tried..." gets better help
3. **Ask for hints** - "Give me a hint, not the full solution"
4. **Use for planning** - "Help me plan my week" saves time
5. **Start early** - Use personas from week 1, not just before exams
